# Marriage Registration in India

## Overview

Marriage registration in India operates under two parallel systems: (1) personal law based registration (Hindu Marriage Act 1955, Muslim personal law, Christian Marriage Act, etc.) and (2) secular registration under the Special Marriage Act 1954 (SMA). The Supreme Court made registration compulsory in *Seema Ghosh vs. State of Rajasthan* (2006).

## Two Systems of Marriage

### Religious Marriage + Registration (HMA 1955)
- Marriage solemnized according to Hindu rites
- Registration is optional but recommended (s. 8)
- Proof of marriage: certificate from Registrar
- Governed by state-specific HMA Registration Rules

### Civil Marriage (SMA 1954)
- Secular marriage — no religious ceremony required
- Notice must be given to Marriage Officer (s. 5)
- 30-day notice period + publication (s. 6-7)
- Objections can be filed (s. 8-9)
- Marriage solemnized in presence of Marriage Officer + 3 witnesses (s. 11, 13)
- Certificate issued immediately (s. 13)

## Conditions for a Valid Marriage

### Under Hindu Marriage Act (s. 5)
- Neither party has a living spouse (monogamy)
- Both capable of giving consent (not unsound mind)
- Bridegroom: minimum 21 years; Bride: minimum 18 years
- Parties not within prohibited degrees (unless custom permits)
- Parties not sapinda (unless custom permits)

### Under Special Marriage Act (s. 4)
- Neither party has a living spouse
- Capable of giving valid consent
- Male: minimum 21 years; Female: minimum 18 years
- Not within prohibited degrees (SMA has wider scope than HMA)

## Registration Procedure

### HMA Registration (ss. 3-8 + State Rules)

1. **Application** → Before Sub-Registrar / Marriage Officer in prescribed form
2. **Documents** → Proof of age, address, photographs, wedding invitation
3. **Verification** → Marriage Officer verifies documents
4. **Declaration** → Both parties declare marriage in presence of Marriage Officer
5. **Witnesses** → 3 witnesses sign the register
6. **Certificate** → Registration certificate issued

### SMA Registration (ss. 5-13)

1. **Notice** → Both parties give notice to Marriage Officer (s. 5)
2. **Publication** → Notice published/displayed for 30 days (s. 6)
3. **Objections** → Anyone can object within 30 days (s. 8-9)
4. **Enquiry** → Marriage Officer may enquire into objections (s. 10)
5. **Solemnization** → "I declare to take ___ as my lawful spouse" (s. 11)
6. **Certificate** → Marriage certificate signed by parties, witnesses, officer (s. 13)

## Documents Required

- Proof of age (Birth certificate / 10th marksheet / Passport)
- Proof of address (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Utility bill)
- Passport size photographs (4-6 copies)
- Affidavit of marital status (unmarried/divorced/widowed)
- Divorce decree (if applicable)
- Death certificate of previous spouse (if widowed)
- Witness identity proofs (with 2-3 witnesses)

## Jurisdictional Aspects

- Marriage registered where: either party resided for 30+ days (SMA s. 5)
- HMA: registered in district of either party or where marriage was solemnized
- NRI marriages: special registration requirements apply
- Foreign marriages: registered at Indian embassy/consulate

## Consequences of Registration

### Under HMA
- Creates presumption of marriage (HMA s. 8(2))
- Admissible as evidence in courts
- Required for passport application (spouse name addition)
- Required for visa applications (dependent visa)

### Under SMA
- Marriage is NOT governed by personal law
- Succession: Indian Succession Act (not personal law)
- Divorce: SMA ss. 27-29 (not HMA)
- Provides legal recognition for inter-caste/inter-religion couples
- Registration is the ONLY proof — no ceremony required

## Divorce

### Under HMA
- s. 13: Fault grounds (adultery, cruelty, desertion, conversion, etc.)
- s. 13B: Divorce by mutual consent
- Minimum 1 year of marriage (s. 14, may be waived in exceptional cases)

### Under SMA
- s. 27: Fault grounds (similar to HMA s. 13)
- s. 28: Divorce by mutual consent
- s. 29: Remarriage of divorced persons
- No waiting period if mutual consent

## Common Situations

### Inter-Caste / Inter-Religious Marriage
- Register under SMA for full legal validity
- SMA provides protection from personal law objections
- Police protection may be needed if family opposes

### NRI Marriage Registration
- Register at Indian consulate/embassy in country of residence
- Also register in India under SMA for validity in Indian courts
- NRI marriages have special provisions for divorce jurisdiction

## Limitations

- HMA only applies to Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs
- SMA has 30-day notice period (can lead to harassment/interference)
- Muslim marriages: governed by Muslim Personal Law (Shariat)
- Christian/Parse marriages: separate statutes exist
- Live-in relationships: NOT registered as marriage
