# Cyber Crimes in India

## Overview

Cyber crimes in India are governed primarily by the Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 (BNS). The IT Act provides for offences related to computers, networks, and digital data, while BNS covers traditional crimes committed through digital means. Investigation follows BNSS 2023 procedure.

## Types of Cyber Crimes

### Hacking (IT Act s. 43, 66)
- Unauthorized access to computer system/network
- Data theft, deletion, modification, or damage
- Punishment: imprisonment up to 3 years + fine up to ₹5 lakh

### Identity Theft (IT Act s. 66C)
- Fraudulently using another person's identity (password, digital signature)
- Punishment: imprisonment up to 3 years + fine up to ₹1 lakh

### Phishing / Vishing (BNS s. 316, IT Act s. 66D)
- Fraudulently obtaining sensitive information (bank details, passwords)
- Impersonation through fake websites/emails/calls
- Punishment under BNS: imprisonment up to 3 years
- Punishment under IT Act: imprisonment up to 3 years + fine

### Cyber Stalking / Harassment (BNS s. 78, IT Act s. 67)
- Online harassment, stalking, threatening messages
- BNS s. 78: Stalking (including cyber stalking)
- Publishing obscene material: IT Act s. 67 (up to 5 years)

### Online Fraud / Cheating (BNS s. 316-318)
- Online shopping fraud, investment scams, romance scams
- BNS s. 318: Cheating by personation using computer/communication device
- Punishment: up to 3 years + fine

### Data Theft / Breach (IT Act s. 43B, 72A)
- Unauthorized access to personal data
- Breach of confidentiality by service provider
- Compensation for wrongful loss

### Cyber Defamation (BNS s. 356)
- Defamation through social media, emails, websites
- Punishment: simple imprisonment up to 2 years + fine

### Ransomware / Cyber Extortion (BNS s. 308)
- Encrypting victim's data and demanding ransom
- BNS s. 308: Extortion (including digital)
- IT Act s. 43: Damage to computer system

## Investigation and Procedure

1. **FIR Registration** → File at local police station (cyber crime wing)
2. **National Cyber Crime Portal** → Report at cybercrime.gov.in
3. **Digital Evidence Preservation** → DO NOT format/delete data
4. **Forensic Analysis** → CFSL / Cyber forensic lab
5. **Arrest / Notice** → Under BNSS provisions
6. **Charge Sheet** → Under IT Act / BNS / BNSS
7. **Trial** → Before competent magistrate/court

## Jurisdictional Issues

- Cyber crimes have extraterritorial jurisdiction (IT Act s. 75)
- Offence can be tried where: computer was accessed, damage occurred, or accused is found
- Supreme Court: "place of cause of action" includes victim's location

## Relevant Provisions

- **IT Act s. 43**: Penalty for damage to computer system
- **IT Act s. 66**: Computer-related offences
- **IT Act s. 66B**: Receiving stolen computer resource
- **IT Act s. 66C**: Identity theft
- **IT Act s. 66D**: Cheating by personation using computer
- **IT Act s. 67-69**: Obscene/sexually explicit content
- **IT Act s. 72-72A**: Breach of confidentiality
- **BNS s. 78**: Stalking (includes cyber stalking)
- **BNS s. 316-318**: Cheating by personation
- **BNS s. 356**: Defamation
- **BNS s. 111-113**: Criminal intimidation
- **DPDP Act 2023**: Data protection obligations

## Common Situations

### Online Banking Fraud
- Immediately report to bank + file complaint on cybercrime.gov.in
- Freeze account under BNSS s. 190 (attachment of property)
- File FIR under IT Act s. 66D + BNS s. 316

### Social Media Impersonation
- Report to platform (report profile)
- File complaint under IT Act s. 66C
- Court order for IP/device tracing

### Revenge Porn / Non-Consensual Intimate Images
- IT Act s. 67A (publishing sexually explicit material)
- BNS s. 77 (voyeurism)
- File FIR, seek interim protection

## Exceptions / Limitations

- IT Act does NOT apply to physical theft of hardware (BNS applies)
- Service providers immune from liability for third-party content (IT Act s. 79, safe harbour)
- Encryption tools: can be regulated under IT Act
- Investigation across states requires coordination
